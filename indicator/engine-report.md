# Native indicator engine

Status: the final D=64 rig and 0.005 agreement deadband passed all **19 checks** of a fresh native Studio probe in RoAlgo: **126 bars, 1,512 completed steps, zero corrupt steps, and a maximum reset-repeat difference of zero**. Both released tails correctly report neutral agreement. Evidence is saved in `results/RoAlgo_native_final_probe_20261005.json` with installed source hashes and Studio version. The original missing-Engine check was observed failing before implementation. Engine, copied stepper, and probe compile with Luau 0.741; the copied stepping implementation matches its frozen source after removing two provenance comment lines.

The rig has three independent masses on passive X-axis `PrismaticConstraint` rails. Each mass connects to a fixed anchor through one native `SpringConstraint` and receives a world-space `VectorForce`. All three receive the same signed drive. Roblox integrates position and velocity. Luau only sets the held force and reads the native state; construction and reset are the only places that place the masses or clear their velocities.

These constants are fixed before market evaluation, not selected from returns. The half-response is the continuous critical-damping expectation from `(1 + omega*t)*exp(-omega*t) = 0.5`, with 0.2 simulated seconds per bar; it is a design description, not a claim of an exact Roblox solver response. This rig uses the lower portion of the old lab's 2–12 radians/s range, extends below it for the slow channel, and fixes critical damping so all three channels retain interpretable multi-bar memory without intentional ringing. Fast/medium/slow describe relative response rates within this rig.

For measured assembly mass `m`, each channel has `k = m*omega^2` and `c = 2*m*omega`. Drive is finite, clamped to [-2, 2], and mapped to `F_x = k*D*drive`, where `D = 64 studs`. A constant drive of +1 therefore has a static displacement target of +64 studs, or normalized displacement +1, for every channel. The spring starts 512 studs long, the rail limit is ±224 studs (±3.5 normalized units), and the fixed anchor is on the negative-X side. The rail guide is 448 studs long. Permitted motion cannot cross the anchor. Bodies remain 1.5-stud cubes with measured mass 3.375; scale changes do not change mass, angular frequency, or damping ratio. Bodies have no collisions, touches, or queries. The default rig origin is `(0, 60, 300)`, away from the prior lab replay.

The physical scale is based on native diagnostics, not market outcomes. At D=1, held drive +1 stalled with fast/medium/slow readings approximately 0.99006/0.94863/0.64183, and the slow channel retained 0.53581 after 30 held bars followed by 40 zero-drive bars. At D=8, the same study measured held readings 0.998885/0.994385/0.974165 and released readings 0.001112/0.005582/0.026057. Increasing physical scale reduced the observed native resting-motion floor in normalized units; merely waiting longer at D=1 did not remove it. The D=64 full probe subsequently measured slow displacement 0.90829 after 16 held bars, 0.0029072 after 40 zero-drive release bars, and 0.0033511 at the separate impulse tail. These observations are consistent with solver quantization/deactivation; the engine's internal deactivation implementation was not inspected.

The six outputs have explicit normalization:

- `fast`, `medium`, `slow`: `q_i = (native_position_x - rest_x)/D`; dimensionless, in drive units.
- `velocity`: fast channel `native_velocity_x/(D*omega_fast)`; signed, dimensionless. Multiply by `D*omega_fast` for studs/s.
- `energy`: the mean of `0.5*(q_i^2 + [native_velocity_x/(D*omega_i)]^2)` across three channels. This is elastic plus kinetic energy divided by each channel's `k_i*D^2`, then averaged. It is a dimensionless state-energy proxy, not market volume or financial energy.
- `agreement`: the mean sign of the three normalized displacements, treating absolute displacement ≤0.005 as neutral. This deadband reflects the measured native resting floor: the previous 0.000001 threshold incorrectly reported agreement +1 at both near-zero tails. +1 means all three above the positive threshold, -1 all below the negative threshold, and 0 includes all-neutral or balanced signs. Raw displacement and velocity values are not clamped or replaced. This is directional agreement, not a probability.

`VerifiedStepper.luau` is copied from `lab/src/Physics/ExplicitRunner.luau` with a provenance comment; its stepping implementation is preserved. The adapter passes exactly the three moving masses plus two metronomes to `workspace:StepPhysics(1/60, parts)`. Anchors and unrelated scene parts are excluded. Every successful bar requires exactly 12 completed steps, confirmed independently by both metronome velocity increments. Dropped requests are reissued with the inherited bound of 30 reissues; non-unit or disagreeing increments stop the call. The adapter refuses further input after a stepping failure until explicit reset, so a caller cannot silently continue a corrupted segment. Reset uses the known equilibrium and clears forces/velocities; it does not add settling steps.

The metronomes are inherited 8-stud free-falling bodies, relaunched every 12 completed steps at ±400 X, Y=5000, Z=-500. This inherited protocol confirms elapsed request time, not the solver's per-assembly internal substep schedule. The native probe separately measures motion and repeatability. The protocol requires Edit mode and positive Workspace gravity. Its 20 initial warmup frames and frame-paced requests mean runtime depends on Studio focus and frame rate.

`EngineProbe.run(parent, options?)` runs 126 bars / 1,512 completed steps in bounded loops and always cleans up its own fixture. An optional `onProgress(completed, 126)` callback supports asynchronous integration. Checks cover finite restrained states, nonzero ordered positive response, held positive/negative signs, approach to normalized unit displacement, signed symmetry, held-input release, native impulse decay, neutral agreement at both released tails, reset sequence agreement within 0.0002 absolute units, exact native object/step counts, rail drift, and an unstepped sentinel outside the engine. Missing Engine installation is an explicit failed check. Both tails after 40 zero-drive bars must have absolute channel displacement below 0.005 and energy below 0.1% of their initial normalized energy. All19 checks passed on the final source. No software integration or mock state is used by this probe.

Integration should call `reset()` at every daily/missing-bar boundary and discard or replay the entire affected segment if `step()` raises. Counters remain cumulative through reset. `destroy()` removes only this engine's model and metronomes.
