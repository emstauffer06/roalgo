# Joint native and numerical mechanics v2

Scope: historical exploratory research; earlier market date ranges have already been inspected. This mechanics probe uses synthetic drives, no market dataset, no train/validation/evaluation split, and no profitability claim. Data hash and split boundaries are therefore not applicable to the probe; the dataset/controller report must supply them for market runs.

## Interfaces and provenance

`Engine.new(workspaceOrDescendant, {name?, origin?})` creates `.model`. `:step(drive8, reset?)` returns `{coupled, independent, numeric}`. `:reset()`, `:stats()`, and `:destroy()` manage the fixture. Native state is never replaced by numerical state. Both rigs implement `movingParts/reset/drive/read` and share one `VerifiedStepper.new({coupled, independent}, {dt=1/60, stepsPerBar=12, settleSteps=0, parent=model})`. Its selected part list contains exactly 48 moving node bodies plus two metronomes; anchored guides and anchors and unrelated Workspace parts are excluded.

The 51-value state order is 24 measured X displacements divided by D, then 24 measured X velocities divided by D times each node's omega, then the Fast/Medium/Slow bank energies. `names` provides that ordering; `banks` contains name, average displacement/velocity, and energy; `nodes` contains normalized x/v, bank index, held projected drive, and modeled net X force in native force units. Force is a diagnostic derived from the recorded motion and continuous force equation, rather than a native solver force measurement. Energy is also derived from recorded motion. Model attributes `FocusPosition` and `ViewPosition` frame the coupled grid while excluding metronomes.

`VerifiedStepper.luau` initially was a byte-for-byte copy of `indicator/src/VerifiedStepper.luau`, itself copied from `lab/src/Physics/ExplicitRunner.luau`. After long-run evidence invalidated its one-frame loss assumption, v2 became an explicitly documented fork: three-frame loss observation, two request-free frames and cumulative checks before any bar readout. Original lab/indicator sources remain unchanged. Corrupt/nonunit steps raise rather than silently counting; a failed Engine step faults the engine until explicit reset/replay. Native and numeric intervals remain one fixed 0.2-second duration per observed market bar, never real overnight time. Waiting wall-clock frames does not add simulated intervals.

## Geometry, parameters, and continuous equations

`Mechanics.config()` is the complete serializable parameter/schema definition (`roalgo-mechanics-v2-1`). It contains 24 nodes in three banks of eight, 21 within-bank neighbor edges and 16 cross-bank neighbor edges, hence 37 coupling edges. The independent native rig has zero coupling edges and identical axial mechanics and inputs. Node bodies retain size 1.5 studs and density 1. Native coefficients use actual `Part.AssemblyMass`, checked identical across the rigs; nominal CLI mass is 3.375. Native config records the actual mass source and every node/edge coefficient.

- D=64 studs per drive unit; row/column spacing=256; coupling free length=192; axial anchor/free length=512; passive rail limits=+/-224 studs; max spring force=1,000,000.
- Fast/Medium/Slow base omega=5/2.5/1.25 rad/s, multiplied by `0.85+0.30*(column-1)/7`. Damping ratio is `0.8+0.35*((column-1)%4)/3`. Axial k=m*omega^2 and c=2*zeta*m*omega.
- Edge k=.18*sqrt(k_a*k_b); edge c=.08*sqrt(c_a*c_b). Every edge explicitly records endpoint indices, initial Y/Z offsets, transverse length, free length, stiffness, damping, and force cap.
- Each input is finite and clamped to [-2,2]. Fixed projection rows have L1 norm 1: matching timescale input has weight .72, with deterministic signed .12/.08/.08 extras. Fast primarily sees return/range/volume; Medium primarily trend/peer/hourly; Slow primarily daily/stress/trend. No learned projection or physical parameter search occurs.

For physical relative X displacement dx=x_b-x_a and relative X velocity dv=v_b-v_a, let L=sqrt(transverse^2+dx^2), direction=dx/L, and lengthRate=dv*direction. Force on endpoint a is `clamp(k*(L-L0)+c*lengthRate, -maxForce, maxForce)*direction`; b receives the opposite force. The axial term is `clamp(-k*x-c*v, -maxForce, maxForce)`, and external drive force is `k*D*q`. The scaled geometry preserves the old spacing4/freeLength3 pretension: small-signal axial coupling tangent is .25*k, with excursion-dependent stiffness. Using the old literal free length3 in D64 geometry would change that model and is deliberately excluded.

Bank energy is the average of each node's physical energy divided by m*omega^2*D^2. Physical energy includes .5*k*x^2 + .5*m*v^2 plus half each adjacent edge's potential increase above its pretension reference: `.5*k*((L-L0)^2-(transverse-L0)^2)`. The total `energy` is the average of three bank energies. All reports retain measured x/v with no sleep-floor deadband.

Numerical integration uses classical fourth-order Runge-Kutta with four internal 1/240-second substeps for each native 1/60-second interval, 12 intervals per bar. It models the same continuous forces, using measured native masses when called by Engine. Rail overflow clamps position and clears outward velocity with restitution zero; safe bounded test inputs never touch that branch. Roblox's native iterative/adaptive constraint solver is a separate integrator and finite-precision implementation. Agreement is assessed with observed deviations; bitwise identity is neither expected nor claimed.

The prevention fork waits up to three frames before reissuing a zero-increment request. Every accepted bar then requires two request-free frames, with both cumulative metronome counts checked immediately after the 12th completed interval and after each quiet frame, before the rig readout or totalBars increment. A check also occurs before changing the next bar's drive. No tolerance or dt was widened; delayed extra intervals still raise. This is bounded scheduling observation and does not prove that arbitrary future latency is impossible. Corruption still terminates a capture; no corrupted interval is accepted and no native field is replaced by numerical output.
